# 🏃‍♂️ Aero-Runner: 3D Procedural Parkour Speedrunner

A fast-paced, high-momentum 3D parkour platformer built with **React**, **Three.js**, **TypeScript**, and **Tailwind CSS**. 

Chain wall-runs, crouch-slides, supersonic air dashes, and double jumps across procedurally synthesized cyber gauntlets.

---

### ✨ Features

- **Fluid Parkour Mechanics**: 
  - Wall-running with dynamic camera tilt and 45° angle kicks
  - Momentum-preserving crouch slides down slopes & under laser grids
  - Hypersonic directional air dashes with radial speed-distortion lines
  - Edge mantling, double-jumping, and dynamic velocity scaling
- **Procedural Track Synthesizer**: Generate infinite unique courses via custom seeds, lengths, and difficulty curves.
- **Holographic Ghost Racing**: Record your runs and race in real-time against your personal best times.
- **Dynamic Camera Perspective**: Toggle between immersive First-Person (FPS) and acrobatic Third-Person chase cameras.
- **Procedural Synthwave Audio**: Adaptive Web Audio synthesizer that escalates in tempo and intensity as your speed and flow multiplier rise.
- **Full Device Support**: Pointer-lock desktop mouse & keyboard controls + responsive touch joysticks for mobile devices.
